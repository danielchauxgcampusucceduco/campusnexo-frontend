### Design specificity verdict

**LLM assessment:** mostly authored for this product. The "drafting sheet" (graphite on cool paper with night-dark viewports), Archivo in expanded and condensed widths, and one status palette shared by every view are deliberate decisions, and the isometric map, the hexagon and the pixelated camera belong to this domain. It slips back to a generic dashboard in Energy (KPI hero + Recharts area/bars + table) and in the scenario dock (identical outline chips). The concept works up close at 1920; at projector distance its most distinctive pieces are the first to stop being readable.

**Deterministic scan:** the static scan of `src` comes out clean (exit 0) with 1 advisory: `codex-grid-background` on `.sheet-grid` (`styles.css:63`), a class no component uses (dead CSS, a false positive). The rendered scan at 1280×720 finds 42 findings across the 5 routes:

### Overall impression

The material and the domain transparency are excellent: the Kalman panel with live equations and the architecture trace with real values are exactly what a jury wants to see. But in front of a projector the product fails at its own principle 2. You fire a scenario and the cause, decision and effect are not visible together: the effect happens somewhere else on screen, in a colour nearly identical to the previous one, while the text that explains it is too small. **The biggest opportunity: make each scenario choreograph its own effect.**

### What's working

1. **A coherent material system.** Paper/graphite with dark viewports and one status palette (`styles.css:1-35`, `theme.ts`). ink-soft on paper gives 5.0:1.
2. **Real domain transparency.** `P⁻ = P + Q·Δt` with live numbers, gain bars and the ±2σ band; the trace shows "0/48 personas, reserva SIM-NO-SHOW → LIBERADO_POR_NO_SHOW". It matches the product principle "every number traceable to its rule".
3. **Honesty and accessibility foundations.** "Hora simulada", "Motor físico en el navegador", "(simulado)" on adapters, privacy pixelation; visible focus, `aria-current`, `aria-live` on toasts, `lang="es"`, reduced-motion, and the iso map is reachable by keyboard.

### Priority issues

**[P0] A scenario's effect is not brought into view**
- **Why it matters:** during the defense you press "No-show" and the panel stays on Aula 301. Aula Magna 204 only changes from teal to olive (a 1.14:1 difference), and the toast covers "Decisión del controlador térmico". The jury does not see cause → decision → effect.
- **Fix:** when a scenario runs, select its `spaceId` in the twin and the camera (`App.tsx:61` renders `<ScenarioDock />` without `onRun`; no view reacts to `highlightedTrace`), pulse the block, and replace the toast with a "Causa → Decisión → Efecto" strip built from the trace ("0/48 personas, 15 min de reserva → Liberado por no-show → HVAC bloqueado") with a "Ver recorrido" link.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify` (+ `layout` for the strip)

**[P0] The signature visuals cannot be read on a projector**
- **Why it matters:** at 1280×720 the isometric-map and hexagon labels render at about 6–7 px (`HexagonFlow.tsx:113-114`, fontSize 11/10 in a 1000-unit viewBox drawn about 596 px wide; `IsoCampus.tsx:92`). Unlit sublabels give 3.5:1, ring outlines 1.5:1. Precisely the pieces that prove the architecture are the ones the jury cannot read.
- **Fix:** SVG text at about 18–20 units with fewer words (sublabels only on lit nodes), lit edges at 3–4 units with visible arrowheads. Below 1440 px, move the side panel under the diagram, or add a "modo proyector" that scales type about 1.4× and hides secondary text.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`, then `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] Status meaning relies on colour, with contrast below AA**
- **Why it matters:** "Libre" (#3c9d8f) and "Liberado por no-show" (#7c8b3a) are nearly the same lightness and on the map they differ only by fill. Status text on the sheet fails 4.5:1 (amber 2.1:1, blue 2.8:1, teal 3.1:1, HVAC badges about 2.5:1); the detector counts 18 cases. Desk freed due to abandonment in red reads as a failure when it is the system's success.
- **Fix:** keep the bright inks for fills and create a darker text variant per status (≥4.5:1). Add a pattern or glyph per status on the iso blocks (hatching for no-show, a sign for over-capacity). "Liberado" in green or neutral, not red.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize` (then `audit`)

**[P1] The scenario dock does not scale**
- **Why it matters:** 12 identical chips in one row (2594 px at 1280) with no scroll cue; "Reiniciar todo" and Acaparamiento are out of view; the space IDs are 11 px condensed; the description only appears on hover. It is the presenter's main control.
- **Fix:** group them into 3–4 labelled blocks (Ocupación y Kalman · Clima MPC · Aulas · Biblioteca), pin Reiniciar on the right, add shortcuts 1–9/0 with visible key hints, and show the selected scenario's description in the dock itself.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout` (with `distill`)

**[P2] Gaps in architecture traceability**
- **Why it matters:**
  - The step list is not synced with the lit node (every dot is the same blue, `ArchitectureView.tsx:63-71`).
  - In simulation it lights "PostgresSpace…" and "StompRealtime…" although InMemory runs (`HexagonFlow.tsx:30-32`), which goes against your principle 5 about honesty.
  - Edges cross the domain with no visible direction.
  - "Eventos recientes" fills up with repeated MQTT ticks that bury the scenario traces.
  - "Disparar un evento" does not say what it fires.
- **Fix:**
  - Highlight the current `<li>` together with its node.
  - Name nodes by port and take the adapter from the trace.
  - Use large arrowheads.
  - Pin or filter the scenario traces.
  - Rename the button to "Disparar Clase en curso (AULA-301)".
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable animate` + `/impeccable clarify`

### Questions to consider

- If the jury can only read one sentence per scenario from 6 m, what is it? And why is that sentence not the largest text on screen after the click?
- What if there were a "modo presentación" that, for each scenario, picks the view, the space and the camera on its own, so you narrate while the interface choreographs the cause, the decision and the effect?
- Showing Postgres/Stomp/Esp32 while the simulation runs InMemory: is that honest by your own principle 5? Shouldn't the adapter ring switch when the data source changes?
